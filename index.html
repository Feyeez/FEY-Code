Lamborgini Sesto Elemento

The Lamborghini Sesto Elemento is a limited-edition, track-only supercar known for its extremely lightweight construction using advanced carbon fiber technology.

It features a 5.2-liter V10 engine, all-wheel drive, and can accelerate from (0-100) km/h in just 2.5 seconds.

Production was limited to only 20 units, and it was designed to be an exclusive, high-performance car for track use only, not for road approval.

NISSAN GTR R34 V SPEC II N1

The Nissan Skyline GT-R V-Spec II N1 (R34) was a rare, homologation-focused version of the R34 GT-R, featuring an N1-spec RB26DETT twin-turbo engine, a 6-speed manual transmission, and a more aggressive V-Spec II aesthetic with a carbon fiber bonnet and stiffer suspension.

While the standard V-Spec II had 280 ps, the N1 versions were built with stronger N1 racing engine blocks, pistons, and camshafts, signifying a more hardcore, track-ready foundation for tuners.

Only a very limited number were built, making it one of the most coveted R34 models. with only 18 units produced

Ferrari LaFerrari Aperta

The Ferrari LaFerrari Aperta is the open-top, convertible version of the LaFerrari hypercar, featuring a removable soft top for open-air driving.

Launched to celebrate Ferrari's 70th anniversary, it's a limited-production hybrid hypercar with a powerful V12 engine and an electric motor, producing a combined ~950 horsepower, making it Ferrari's fastest road-going spider. Only 210 units of the Ferrari LaFerrari Aperta were produced

McLaren Senna GTR

The McLaren Senna GTR is a track-only hypercar that is a more powerful and extreme version of the road-legal Senna, producing 825 hp and 800 Nm of torque from its 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 engine.

It generates up to 1,000 kg of downforce, uses a motorsport-derived suspension, and features various performance-oriented upgrades to deliver unparalleled track performance.

It was launched in 2019 with a limited production run of just 75 units.
